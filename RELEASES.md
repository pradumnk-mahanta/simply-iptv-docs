## [0.0.2] - 2026-10-29

* Upgraded Google Play Billing Library to 9.1.0 to comply with Google Play's latest monetization requirements.
* Configured 5-second auto-dismiss timeout for the player bottom channel info bar.
* Optimized and cleaned unused string resources across all 10 language localizations.
* Added official Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions documentation.

---

## [0.0.1] - 2026-10-29

* Initial release of Simply IPTV for Android TV and Google TV.
* Material 3 Leanback TV interface optimized for remote control navigation.
* Multi-playlist management with automatic category grouping and stream resolution tagging.
* XMLTV EPG support with schedule progress and timeline details.
* Seamless stream source switching via the bottom player bar.
* Android TV system home launcher integration ("Continue Watching" / "Watch Next").
